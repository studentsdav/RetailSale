## 1. Overview of A5 Invoice Format

A5 is half the size of standard A4 paper ($148 \times 210\text{ mm}$ or $5.8 \times 8.3\text{ inches}$).

### Why use A5?
- **Saves 50% on paper costs** compared to full A4 sheets.
- **Professional presentation**: Provides itemized product tables, tax breakups, store branding, and barcode/QR codes.
- **Ideal for**: Retail boutiques, apparel shops, clinics, mobile repair stores, and pharmacies.

---

## 2. Setting A5 as Default Invoice Format

1. Go to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **System Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Printing & Templates**.
2. Under **Default Invoice Paper Format**, select **A5 (Half Page)**.
3. Choose the default number of copies (e.g. 1 Copy for Customer, 1 Copy for Store).
4. Click **Save Settings**.

---

## 3. Customizing the A5 Template Design

Navigate to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Template Designer** $\rightarrow$ **A5 Invoice Tab**:

- **Store Header**: Choose Center, Left, or Right alignment.
- **Logo Display**: Toggle store logo visibility ON/OFF.
- **Tax Grid**: Show or hide detailed CGST/SGST/VAT breakups.
- **Bank & UPI Details**: Display store bank account or UPI QR code for fast payment receipts.
- **Color Theme**: Pick your brand accent color (e.g., Emerald Teal, Classic Navy, Crimson).
- **Terms & Footer Note**: Add customized store return policies or warranty terms.

---

## 4. Printing A5 Invoices at Checkout & Reprint

### At Sales Checkout:
- When completing a bill on the POS screen, the Print Dialog defaults to **A5**.
- A high-resolution print preview opens immediately.
- Click **Print** or press `Enter` to send directly to your connected printer.

### On Reprint:
- From the **Sales History / Reprint Bill** screen, click **Reprint**.
- Select **A5** from the format options to generate the half-page invoice.

---

## 5. Paper & Printer Loading Tips

- **Pre-cut A5 Sheets**: Load pre-cut A5 paper into your standard printer paper cassette. Set your printer driver paper size to `A5`.
- **2 Invoices per A4 Sheet**: If using full A4 paper, you can configure your printer to print "2 pages per sheet" for dual half-page receipts.
