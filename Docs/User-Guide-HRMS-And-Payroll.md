## 2. HRMS Masters (Departments, Designations & Shifts)

Before adding employees, configure your organizational structure:

### Step 1: Designations & Departments
1. Go to **HRMS > HRMS Masters**.
2. Add **Departments** (e.g., *Sales Counter*, *Inventory Warehouse*, *Kitchen*, *Accounts*, *Delivery Logistics*).
3. Add **Designations** (e.g., *Head Cashier*, *Store Manager*, *Senior Chef*, *Rider*, *Inventory Executive*).

### Step 2: Work Shifts & Timings
1. In **HRMS Masters**, click **Shifts**.
2. Click **Create Shift ➕**.
3. Set Shift Parameters:
   - **Shift Name**: e.g., *Morning Shift*, *Evening Rush Shift*, *General Shift*.
   - **Start Time & End Time**: e.g., 09:00 AM – 06:00 PM.
   - **Grace Period (mins)**: e.g., 15 minutes allowed for late punch-in without penalty.
   - **Half-Day Minimum Hours**: e.g., 4.5 hours.
4. Click **Save Shift**.

## 3. Employee Management & Profiles

Maintain complete digital records of all staff members:

1. Go to **HRMS > Employee Directory**.
2. Click **Add New Employee ➕**.
3. Fill in Employee Details:
   - **Personal Info**: Full Name, Phone Number, Email, Emergency Contact, Address.
   - **Employment Info**: Employee ID (auto-generated), Joining Date, Department, Designation, Assigned Shift.
   - **Banking / Payout Info**: Bank Name, Account Number, IFSC/Routing code, Payment Mode (*Bank Transfer*, *Cash*, *Cheque*).
   - **Documents**: Upload KYC documents (ID Proof, Photo).
4. Click **Save Employee**.

## 4. Daily Attendance & Punch-In / Punch-Out

Track employee presence, punctuality, and overtime:

### Daily Punch-In / Out
1. Go to **HRMS > Daily Attendance**.
2. Staff members or the store supervisor can select the employee profile or scan employee ID barcode/badge.
3. Click **Punch In** at start of work and **Punch Out** at end of shift.
4. The system calculates:
   - Total working hours.
   - Punctuality status (🟢 *On Time*, 🟡 *Late Arrival*, 🔴 *Half Day / Absent*).
   - Overtime hours worked beyond shift end time.

### Attendance History & Manual Overrides
Supervisors can view monthly attendance grids, correct missed punch-outs, and mark approved field duty with 1 click.

## 5. Leave Types & Staff Leave Requests

1. Configure leave quotas under **HRMS Masters > Leave Types** (e.g., *Casual Leave (CL)*, *Sick Leave (SL)*, *Paid Annual Leave*).
2. When staff request time off:
   - Select Employee profile.
   - Enter Leave Start Date, End Date, and Reason.
   - Click **Approve** or **Reject**. Approved leaves will not incur salary loss.

## 6. Salary Structures & Pay Schedules

Define how staff compensation is structured:

1. Go to **HRMS > Pay Schedules**.
2. Create **Salary Components**:
   - **Earnings**: Basic Salary, House Rent Allowance (HRA), Dearness Allowance (DA), Travel Allowance, Performance Bonus.
   - **Deductions**: Provident Fund (PF), Employee State Insurance (ESI), Professional Tax, Staff Loan/Advance recovery.
3. Assign the Salary Structure to the employee profile with monthly base rate.

---

## 7. Monthly Payroll Processing & Payslips

Run salary calculations at month-end in under 2 minutes:

1. Go to **HRMS > Monthly Payroll Processing**.
2. Select the **Pay Period** (e.g., *October 2026*).
3. Click **Generate Payroll**.
4. The engine automatically pulls:
   - Total days present from Attendance.
   - Overtime hours earned.
   - Approved paid leaves vs unapproved unpaid leaves (Loss of Pay).
   - Base earnings, bonuses, and mandatory deductions.
5. Review the summary table.
6. Click **Approve & Finalize Payroll**.
7. Click **Print Payslips / Export PDF** to distribute professional payslips with company logo to staff.

---

## 8. Staff Advance Payments & Loan Deductions

Manage salary advances given to staff during emergency needs:

1. Go to **Accounting > Accounting Vouchers** or **HRMS > Employee Profile**.
2. Record the **Salary Advance** given to the employee.
3. Set monthly deduction amount (e.g., deduct \$100/month across 3 months).
4. During the next monthly payroll run, the system automatically subtracts the scheduled installment and updates the outstanding advance balance!
