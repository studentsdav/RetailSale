## 1. Overview of the Reports Suite

Access all analytical tools from the left sidebar under **Reports** and **Stock View**:

```text
Reports Hub
├── 📈 Sales & Payments (Sales Report, Payment Analysis, Credit Analysis)
├── 📦 Stock & Audits (Stock Balance, Stock Ledger, Damage Summary, Transfers)
├── 🌙 Day End Operations (Cashier Handover, Day Closing, Night Audit)
├── 🏷️ Marketing & Schemes (Brand Analysis, Scheme Report, Loyalty Report)
├── 🚚 Procurement & Vendors (Purchase Report, Supplier Payments, Return Reports)
└── 👥 Staff & Commissions (Commission Report, Operations Intelligence)
```

## 2. Sales, Revenue & Payment Reports

### Retail Sales & Tax Split Report
- **Screen**: `Reports > Retail Sales Report`
- Filter by date range (Today, This Week, This Month, Custom Date Range), cashier terminal, or outlet.
- Displays: Gross Sales, Net Discounts Applied, Net Sales, Tax Breakdown (CGST, SGST, IGST, VAT), and Final Billed Revenue.

### Payment Mode Analysis (Cash, Card, M-Pesa, UPI)
- **Screen**: `Reports > Payment Analysis`
- Breaks down your revenue by payment channel so you can reconcile bank merchant settlements, card POS swipe machine slips, and mobile money wallets.

### Credit & Aging Analysis
- **Screen**: `Reports > Credit Analysis`
- Lists customer accounts with pending credit balances, sorted by aging buckets (0–30 days, 31–60 days, 60+ days overdue) with 1-click WhatsApp payment reminders!

---

## 3. Stock, Valuation & Transfer Reports

### Live Stock Balance & Valuation
- **Screen**: `Stock View > Stock Balance`
- Displays current on-hand quantities, cost valuation, retail selling valuation, and projected profit margin across all items. Items below minimum stock are highlighted in red.

### Stock Ledger (Item Transaction History)
- **Screen**: `Reports > Stock Ledger Report`
- Audit the complete lifecycle of any product: every purchase inward (+), customer sale (-), inter-branch transfer, damage write-off, and manual stock adjustment with running balance!

### Stock Transfer Progress Dashboard
- **Screen**: `Reports > Transfer Progress Dashboard`
- Track goods in transit between your Central Warehouse and Retail Branch Outlets with live status indicators (Requested ➔ Dispatched ➔ In Transit ➔ Received).

---

## 4. Cashier Handover, Day Closing & Night Audit

### Cashier Shift Handover Report
- **Screen**: `Reports > Cashier Handover Report`
- Run at the end of every cashier shift:
  1. Cashier enters physical cash counted in drawer.
  2. System compares counted cash against recorded cash sales.
  3. Highlights any cash surplus or shortage for manager sign-off.

### Daily Closing Report (Z-Report)
- **Screen**: `Reports > Closing Report`
- Comprehensive end-of-day summary showing total customer footfall, average basket size, hourly sales distribution, and total tender collections.

### Night Audit Wizard
- **Screen**: `Reports > Night Audit`
- Automated midnight audit that checks for unbilled restaurant KOTs, open delivery orders, locks the daily ledger, and generates the certified daily executive summary.

## 5. Marketing, Schemes & Loyalty Reports

- **Brand & Store Category Analysis**: Discover which product brands and departments generate 80% of your store's profits (Pareto Analysis).
- **Scheme & Promo Report**: Compare revenue generated during Happy Hours vs Bill-Value discount campaigns.
- **Loyalty & Lucky Draw Analytics**: Track loyalty point liabilities, active member points balances, and lucky draw raffle participant statistics.

---

## 6. Procurement & Supplier Reports

- **Purchase Report**: Summary of all wholesale vendor orders placed, received, and pending delivery.
- **Supplier Payments Report**: Detailed ledger of payments made to vendors via bank transfer, cheque, or cash drawer.
- **Refund Pending Report**: Track supplier debit notes and pending cash returns for defective goods sent back to vendors.

---

## 7. Sales Commission & Staff Performance

- **Commission Rules & Reports**: Configure tiered commission rates for sales executives (e.g., 2% on electronics, 5% on accessories).
- Automatically calculates monthly commission payouts based on verified completed sales invoices.

## 8. Exporting to PDF, Excel & Direct WhatsApp Sharing

- Every report screen features **1-Click Export buttons**:
  - 📄 **Export PDF**: Generates certified vector PDF reports with your store logo and header.
  - 📊 **Export Excel / CSV**: Exports clean tabular data for external accountant auditing in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
  - 📱 **Send to WhatsApp**: Directly message the daily sales summary to the business owner's mobile phone!
